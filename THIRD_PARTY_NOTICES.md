Existing source notices have been preserved. This inventory is not a substitute
for the upstream license texts and is not a claim that the list is exhaustive.

- `transformercvn/network/layers/sparse_convnext.py` carries a Meta Platforms
  copyright notice and refers to a root `LICENSE` file that was absent from the
  supplied directory. Identify the exact upstream source/revision and restore its
  applicable license before distributing this file.
- `transformercvn/network/layers/prong_mobilenet_embedding.py` and
  `prong_masked_mobilenet_embedding.py` attribute their channel-rounding helper to
  TensorFlow Models; the original source reference remains in their docstrings.
  Confirm the source revision and preserve the required upstream notices.
- Confirm provenance and redistribution terms for the remaining inherited model,
  scheduler and loss code before selecting a repository-wide license.

No repository-wide license has been added by the cleanup, because the original
material did not establish ownership or an intended license.
